FAQ

A few quick answers.

What makes SwapBelt different?

SwapBelt separates the waterproof core from the outer sleeve. One core works across interchangeable sleeves, giving you a bounce-free running belt that keeps your essentials dry while letting you rotate fresh styles.

How do the interchangeable sleeves work?

Insert the waterproof core into the sleeve and use the same core across your rotation. Change colors, patterns, or custom designs whenever you want.

Can I get a custom sleeve for my run club or race?

Yes. Join the early access list and reach out — we work directly with run clubs, marathons, and organizations on custom sleeve designs.

Do I need multiple sleeves?

No. SwapBelt works with a single sleeve, but additional sleeves let you change your style or rotate a fresh sleeve between runs.

What will SwapBelt cost?

We're targeting a launch price around $59 for the core and sleeve set, with additional sleeves sold separately. Early access members get first access to launch pricing.

Is SwapBelt available now?

Not yet. We're currently refining the final production prototype.
