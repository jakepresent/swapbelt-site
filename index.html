How it works

Fresh belt. Same waterproof core.

SwapBelt is designed as a two-part system: a removable TPU pouch that carries your essentials and a washable sleeve that handles sweat.

  1. Insert coreThe pouch slides into the top-entry sleeve opening.
  2. Snap secureSnaps hold the core in position while the sleeve stretches with you.
  3. Run, remove, cleanAfter the run, remove the pouch, wipe it down, and wash the sleeve.
