FAQ

A few quick answers.

What makes SwapBelt different?

SwapBelt separates storage from the belt itself. One waterproof core works across multiple interchangeable belts, so you can keep your essentials protected while rotating clean belts between runs.

Why would I own multiple belts?

Every SwapBelt set includes multiple belt sleeves. Some runners rotate belts after workouts, while others enjoy different colors and styles. SwapBelt is designed for both.

Do I need multiple belts?

No. SwapBelt works with a single belt, but the experience becomes more convenient when you can rotate fresh belts between runs.

Is SwapBelt available now?

Not yet. We're currently refining the final production prototype.
